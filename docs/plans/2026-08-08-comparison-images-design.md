# Comparison Images Design

## Goal

Add the first two visual assets for Droplet's comparison ladder: `drop` and `teaspoon`.

## Direction

The assets will be separate 512 px square PNGs with transparent backgrounds. They use a restrained editorial 3D style aligned with the popup's warm-paper and muted-teal visual language rather than generic line icons or photorealistic stock cutouts.

- **Drop:** a single sculptural dark-teal water droplet.
- **Teaspoon:** a simple matte-silver teaspoon at a gentle angle, containing a small teal water fill.

They will contain no text, logos, cast shadows, scenes, or watermarks. Source renders will use a chroma-key background and be locally converted and checked for clean alpha transparency.

## Delivery

Final project assets will live in `public/comparison-images/drop.png` and `public/comparison-images/teaspoon.png`. They are intentionally not wired into the popup in this asset-only change.
